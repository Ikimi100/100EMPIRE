The short answer

Most pure-breed puppies cost between ₦150,000 and ₦1,500,000 in 2026. Small companion breeds such as the Lhasa Apso and Maltese start from about ₦150,000. A German Shepherd or Rottweiler puppy is ₦300,000 to ₦1,000,000, a Boerboel ₦350,000 to ₦1,200,000 and a Caucasian Shepherd ₦400,000 to ₦1,500,000.

We only deal in pure breeds: both parents are the same recognised breed. Prices are per puppy and change with each litter.

What changes the price

Two puppies of the same breed can be hundreds of thousands of naira apart. These are the reasons.

  • Lineage and papers. Litters registered with COBA, Nigeria’s national kennel club, come with pedigree papers that show the ancestry. They cost more.
  • Imported parents. Puppies from imported or proven parents sit at the top of the range.
  • Sex, colour and coat. Some colours and coat types are rarer, and some buyers pay more for a male or a female.
  • Age. The price changes as a puppy grows. We quote for the puppy’s age on the day.
  • Care already given. Vaccination, deworming and a health check before handover are part of a fair price.

What a puppy should come with

Whoever you buy from, ask for these. Every puppy from us comes with them.

  • Its first vaccination and deworming, written on a vaccination card. Puppies go home from 8 weeks old.
  • A health check before it leaves.
  • Photos and live video of the puppy and both parents before you pay. You can also visit by appointment.
  • Pedigree papers where the litter is registered. We tell you before you pay whether it has papers.
  • A feeding plan, a first-week checklist, and answers on WhatsApp for as long as you have the dog.

How to buy a puppy safely

  • See the puppy and both parents on a live video call or in person, not only in photos.
  • Ask for the vaccination card and check the dates against the vaccination schedule.
  • If a seller mentions papers, ask which kennel club issued them. In Nigeria, pedigree papers come from COBA.
  • Be careful with prices far below the range for that breed, and with sellers who will not show the parents.
  • Do not pay the full price to a seller you cannot verify.

Costs after you buy

The purchase price is only the start. Plan for food, vaccination boosters, deworming, vet visits, grooming for long-coated breeds, and shade or cooling for breeds that struggle in the heat, such as the Husky, Chow Chow and English Bulldog.

Our puppy vaccination calculator gives you every vaccination date from your puppy’s birthday. A breed consultation also covers what a breed costs to feed and care for.

Delivery and pick-up

We deliver nationwide, to any state in Nigeria, or hand your puppy over at a pick-up point that suits you. Delivery is priced by distance and quoted together with the puppy.

Questions people ask

How much is a German Shepherd puppy in Nigeria?

In 2026 a pure-bred German Shepherd puppy costs ₦300,000 to ₦1,000,000. Puppies from registered or imported parents, with pedigree papers, sit at the top of that range.

How much is a Boerboel puppy in Nigeria?

₦350,000 to ₦1,200,000 in 2026. A Caucasian Shepherd, the other giant guard breed, is ₦400,000 to ₦1,500,000.

Which pure breed costs the least?

Small companion breeds. The Lhasa Apso starts from about ₦150,000 and the Maltese costs ₦150,000 to ₦300,000.

Why are prices shown as a range?

Price depends on lineage, papers, imported parents, age, sex and colour. Ask for the exact price of an available puppy.

When can a puppy go home?

From 8 weeks old, after its first vaccination and deworming. Younger puppies stay with their mother.

Do you deliver outside Abuja?

Yes, nationwide. We deliver to any state in Nigeria, or hand your puppy over at a pick-up point that suits you. The delivery cost depends on distance and is quoted with the puppy.

Looking for a puppy?

Tell us the breed, your state and when you want the puppy. We send photos and videos of available puppies and their parents.
