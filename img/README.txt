PHOTOS
======

1. YOUR CUT-OUT PORTRAIT (homepage hero + About page)
   ikimi.webp and ikimi.png are the photo of you with the background removed.
   To change it, replace both files with a new cut-out (transparent background,
   upright, about 600 x 1250 px). Keep the same file names.

2. SERVICE PHOTOS
   The service pictures are free stock photos from Pexels (free to use under
   the Pexels licence, no credit required). They load straight from Pexels,
   so they need internet and show on the live site.

   To use your OWN work instead (recommended over time), send the photos to
   Claude and ask for them to be swapped in, one per service:
   graphic design, video editing, photography, weddings, church media,
   web design, apps, social media, books, academy, frames, dogs.
