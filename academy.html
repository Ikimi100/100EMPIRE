Questions

Before you book.

I am a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes. Every course starts from the basics. The web development course is written in very plain language for people who have never coded.

Do I need a laptop?

For design, editing, motion and web classes, yes. Photography can start with a phone. Mini importation and social media need only a smartphone.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Pay half to start and the rest by the middle of the course.

Are classes online or physical?

Both. Physical classes hold at our Suleja studio; online classes run on Zoom and WhatsApp.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, after completing the course projects.
