The schedule behind the dates

Based on the 2024 vaccination guidelines of the World Small Animal Veterinary Association (WSAVA). Your vet confirms the exact plan for each dog.

AgeWhat is given
From 2 weeksDeworming. Repeated regularly through puppyhood and every few months for adults.
6–8 weeksCore vaccine, 1st dose. Distemper, hepatitis (adenovirus) and parvovirus.
10–12 weeksCore vaccine, 2nd dose. Leptospirosis can start here if your vet advises it.
14–16 weeksFinal puppy dose and rabies. The last puppy dose is given at 16 weeks or older. Rabies is present in Nigeria, so it is a core vaccine here.
6–12 monthsBooster. Closes any gap left by the puppy course.
Adult dogsBoosters. Core vaccines no more often than every 3 years. Rabies and leptospirosis as your vet advises.

Questions people ask

When should a puppy get its first vaccine?

At 6–8 weeks old, with the core vaccine against distemper, hepatitis (adenovirus) and parvovirus. Deworming starts from 2 weeks.

Why must the last puppy dose be at 16 weeks or older?

Antibodies from the mother can stop earlier doses from working. Giving the final puppy dose at 16 weeks or older makes sure it takes.

Does my puppy need the rabies vaccine?

Yes. Rabies is present in Nigeria, so rabies is treated as a core vaccine here. It is given with the final puppy dose.

How often do adult dogs need boosters?

Core vaccines no more often than every 3 years after the 6–12 month booster. Rabies and leptospirosis follow your vet’s advice.

My puppy missed a dose. What now?

Speak to your vet. Depending on the gap, the vet may continue the course or restart it.
