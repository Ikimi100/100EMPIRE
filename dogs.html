Vaccination guide

The international schedule.

Based on the 2024 vaccination guidelines of the World Small Animal Veterinary Association (WSAVA). Your vet confirms the exact plan for each dog.

Puppy and adult dog vaccination schedule
AgeWhat is given
From 2 weeksDewormingRepeated regularly through puppyhood and every few months for adults. Each dose goes on the card.
6–8 weeksCore vaccine, 1st doseDistemper, hepatitis (adenovirus) and parvovirus.
10–12 weeksCore vaccine, 2nd doseLeptospirosis can start here if your vet advises it.
14–16 weeksCore vaccine, final puppy dose + rabiesThe last puppy dose is given at 16 weeks or older. Rabies is present in Nigeria, so it is a core vaccine here.
6–12 monthsBoosterCloses any gap left by the puppy course.
Adult dogsBoostersCore vaccines no more often than every 3 years. Rabies and leptospirosis as your vet advises.

Questions

Before you book.

Do you sell mixed or local breeds?

No. We only deal in pure breeds: both parents are the same recognised breed. If you want a local dog, we can still advise you on care and vaccination.

How do I know a puppy is pure-bred?

You see the puppy and both parents on photos and live video before you pay, or visit by appointment. Where a litter is registered, its pedigree papers show the ancestry.

Do your puppies come with pedigree papers?

Some litters do. Papers in Nigeria come from COBA, the Canine Owners and Breeders Association, which the FCI recognises as Nigeria’s national kennel club and whose pedigrees the American Kennel Club accepts. We tell you before you pay whether a litter has papers.

Why are prices shown as a range?

Price depends on lineage, papers, imported parents, age, sex and colour. The guide shows typical Nigerian prices in 2026. Ask for the exact price of an available puppy.

When can a puppy go home?

From 8 weeks old, after its first vaccination and deworming. Younger puppies stay with their mother.

Can you mate my dog with one of yours?

Yes, for pure breeds only. Both dogs must be adult, healthy and vaccinated, and of the same breed. We check papers and temperament before agreeing a mating. Ask for terms.

Do you deliver?

Pick-up is in Suleja. Delivery within Abuja and nearby can be arranged at your cost.
