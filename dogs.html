Questions

Before you book.

Do your puppies come with papers?

Some litters come with pedigree papers. Every puppy comes with its vaccination and deworming record. Ask about papers for a specific litter.

Can I see the puppy before paying?

Yes. We send photos and live video, and you can visit by appointment.

Which breed is right for me?

Tell us about your home, compound, family and time. We will recommend breeds that fit, including ones we do not currently have.

Do you deliver?

Pick-up is in Suleja. Delivery within Abuja and nearby can be arranged at your cost.

Looking for a puppy?

Tell us the breed and when you want it. We will send what is available.
