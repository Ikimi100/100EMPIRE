Questions

Before you book.

Is ad spend included?

No. You pay the platform directly with your own card or account. Our fee covers strategy, creatives and management.

How soon will I see results?

Consistent posting shows movement within the first month. Ads can bring enquiries within days. Most clients judge results after three months.

Do I approve posts before they go out?

Yes. You get the month's content calendar in advance and can approve or change anything.

Do you work on political and civic campaigns?

Yes. We plan campaign media and grassroots digital structures. Each campaign is scoped and quoted on its own brief.

Who owns the pages?

You do. We work as admins on your accounts and never as owners.
