How I charge

Simple,
written terms.

  • QuotesEvery job gets a written quote on WhatsApp or email before work starts. A price range becomes one fixed figure in your quote.
  • DepositsDesign jobs: 70% to start. Video one-offs: 60%. Weddings and shoots: 50% to book the date. Websites and apps: 50%, 30% and 20% at milestones.
  • RevisionsEach package lists its revision rounds. Extra rounds are charged for design and video, or quoted for builds.
  • Rush workDesign delivered in under 24 hours and rush video edits attract a rush fee.
  • TravelSuleja and Abuja are covered. Other locations attract transport and, where needed, accommodation at cost.
  • Third-party costsPrinting, ad spend, domains, hosting, app store fees and equipment are paid separately or passed on at cost.
  • OwnershipFinal files, domains, code and accounts are yours once the balance is paid.
  • Price changesPrices are reviewed every quarter. A quote you have accepted keeps its price for the agreed timeline.
