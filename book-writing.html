Questions

Before you book.

Will my name be on the book?

Yes. Ghostwriting is confidential. The book is yours and we never claim credit or name our ghostwriting clients.

How do you capture my voice?

Through recorded interviews, your sermons or talks, and samples of your writing. You review every chapter.

Can you work from sermons or voice notes?

Yes. That is one of our most common projects. We transcribe, organise and write the material into chapters.

Do you help with printing?

Yes. We prepare print-ready files and connect you with printers for local runs, or publish to Amazon KDP and Selar.

How do payments work?

Ghostwriting is paid in three milestones. Editing is quoted per word after we see a sample of the manuscript.
