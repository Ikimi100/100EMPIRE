How to use it

  • Enter your total budget and the number of guests.
  • Fill in an estimate for each item. Delete what you do not need and add your own items.
  • As you pay vendors, enter what you have paid. The planner shows what is still to pay.
  • For photo and video, pick a 100EMPIRE package to fill in its price, or type your own figure.

Prices for photo, video, albums and stationery are in our wedding photo and film price guide.

Questions people ask

Where do I start with a wedding budget?

Start with the guest count. Food, drinks, the size of the venue, chairs, souvenirs and aso ebi all grow with it. Then fill in the big items first: venue, food and drinks, photo and video, and decoration.

Should I keep money aside for surprises?

Yes. The planner has a line for unexpected costs. Fill it in so a late bill does not come out of something you care about.

Is my budget saved?

Yes, on this phone or computer only, in your browser. We cannot see it. Use Send to WhatsApp or Save as PDF to share it with your partner or planner.
