What happens after you book

  1. Audit. We review your pages, competitors and current results.
  2. Plan. Goals, audience, content pillars and a monthly calendar.
  3. Create. Designs, reels and captions prepared in advance. You approve posts before they go out.
  4. Publish. Scheduled posting, replies and ads running.
  5. Report. Monthly numbers, what worked and next month’s plan.

Questions people ask

Is ad spend included?

No. You pay the platform directly with your own card or account. Our fee covers strategy, creatives and management.

What if I need more platforms than my plan includes?

Pick them anyway. Each platform beyond your plan’s number is ₦70,000 a month, and the total updates as you choose.

Do I approve posts before they go out?

Yes. You get the month’s content calendar in advance and can approve or change anything.

Who owns the pages?

You do. We work as admins on your accounts and never as owners.

Do you work on political and civic campaigns?

Yes. Campaign media is scoped and quoted on its own brief, from ₦1,000,000 a month. Send your brief on WhatsApp.
