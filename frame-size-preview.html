Questions people ask

Which frame size suits a living room?

24×30 inches and above work well over a sofa. 16×20 suits bedrooms and offices.

Is the preview exact?

It is drawn to scale, so the size against the sofa and the person is right. Colours on screen and the exact crop of your photo are confirmed before printing.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The preview runs on your phone or computer. When you order, send the original photo on WhatsApp so we can check it is sharp enough for the size.

Can you frame a photo from my phone?

Yes. Send the original photo on WhatsApp (not a screenshot). We check if it is sharp enough for the size you want and advise you.
