Questions

Before you book.

Android or iOS first?

Most Nigerian users are on Android, so we usually launch there first. Because we use React Native, the iOS version comes from the same code.

Do I need my own developer accounts?

Yes. Store accounts should be in your organisation's name. We help you create them and handle the publishing.

Who owns the code?

You do, once the final milestone is paid. We hand over the repository and every login.

Can you turn my website into an app?

Yes. App lite is the fastest route. For a dashboard-style app with sign-in, choose Starter or Business.

What happens after launch?

Phones and app stores change every year. A maintenance plan keeps your app working, secure and up to date.
