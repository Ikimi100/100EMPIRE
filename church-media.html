Livestream kits

Start where your budget is.

We size the setup to your hall, your team and your budget, then train the people who will run it.

Starter

One good camera or phone, a capture device, a laptop running OBS and a direct audio feed from your mixer.

Standard

Two to three cameras, a hardware switcher, lower thirds and scripture slides, and a dedicated streaming laptop.

Pro

Multi-camera with PTZ cameras, an audio mixer for the stream, recording for edits and a trained crew.

Your team

Every setup ends with hands-on training and a simple checklist your volunteers can follow on Sunday morning.

Questions

Before you book.

We are a small church. Is this for us?

Yes. The Basic plan was designed for small and growing churches, and we can start with only graphics or only sermon clips.

Do we have to buy equipment?

For livestreaming, yes. We give you a costed list for your budget and install it. For graphics and editing, you only need someone to send us the material.

Can you train our volunteers?

Yes. Training is included in the Gold plan and available on its own for any church.

Do you manage our YouTube and Facebook pages?

From the Premium plan up, we post, write captions and respond to comments. You keep ownership of every account.

How long is the contract?

Three months to start, then month to month with 30 days’ notice.
