Questions

Before you book.

Do I own the design?

Yes. Once the balance is paid, the final design is yours to use anywhere. Editable source files are available for an added fee.

What should I send you?

The text you want on the design, your logo, any photos, the sizes or where it will be posted, and your deadline. A voice note is fine if that is easier.

How fast can you deliver?

Single flyers are delivered in 24 hours. Same-day delivery is possible for an extra 40% when the schedule allows.

Can you print and frame?

Yes. We print flyers, banners, cards and stickers through trusted printers and frame at our Suleja studio. Printing is quoted after you approve the design.

Can you redesign my old logo?

Yes. We can refresh an existing logo so it keeps its recognition, or build a new one. Redesigns are priced like the Standard logo package.

What if I do not like the first concepts?

Tell us what feels wrong. Revisions are included in every package, and on the Standard logo you get three directions to start from.
