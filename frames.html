Questions

Before you book.

Can you frame a photo from my phone?

Yes. Send the original photo on WhatsApp (not a screenshot). We check if it is sharp enough for the size you want and advise you.

Which size is best for a living room?

24×30 inches and above work well over a sofa. 16×20 suits bedrooms and offices. We can send a mock-up on your wall photo.

Do you do bulk orders?

Yes. Schools, churches and companies get a discount on 10 pieces or more. Ask for a bulk quote.

How do I collect my frame?

Pick up at No 1 Hali Plaza, Kwata, Suleja, or ask us to arrange delivery at your cost.
