The short answer

A framed 8×10 photo costs ₦8,000 with a border frame, ₦10,000 borderless and ₦15,000 in acrylic. A 16×20 is ₦20,000 to ₦40,000 and a 24×30 ₦40,000 to ₦60,000. The largest size, 7×9 feet, is ₦190,000 to ₦250,000. These are 100EMPIRE’s 2026 prices per framed piece.

Border, borderless or acrylic?

  • Border frame. A classic framed finish with a visible border around your print. Warm, traditional and the most affordable.
  • Borderless frame. An edge-to-edge print with no visible border. Clean and modern for living rooms and offices.
  • Acrylic frame. A premium glossy acrylic face-mount with deep colour and a high-end finish.

Which size to choose

  • 8×10 and 10×12. Desks, shelves and side tables.
  • 16×20. Bedrooms, offices and corridors.
  • 24×30 and above. Over a sofa or as the main piece in a living room.
  • 5×7 ft and 7×9 ft. Statement pieces for halls, churches, offices and events.

Questions people ask

How much is a 16×20 frame in Abuja?

₦20,000 with a border frame, ₦25,000 borderless and ₦40,000 in acrylic.

How much is a 24×30 frame?

₦40,000 with a border frame, ₦45,000 borderless and ₦60,000 in acrylic. 24×30 and above work well over a sofa.
