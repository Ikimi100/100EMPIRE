Questions

Before you book.

Do we get the raw files?

Every package includes edited photos. Raw, unedited files are available for ₦20,000 per session if you need them.

What should I wear?

Solid colours and outfits that fit well photograph best. We send a short guide after booking.

Can you restore an old family photo?

Yes. Scan it or bring it to the studio. We repair tears, fading and stains, then print and frame it if you want.

Do you travel?

Yes. Suleja and Abuja are covered in the package price. Other locations attract transport and, where needed, accommodation.
