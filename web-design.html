Questions

Before you book.

Can I update the website myself?

From the Growth package up, yes. We set up an editor and show you how to change text, photos, events and listings.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You do. We register the domain in your name and hand over the code and logins when the final payment is made.

How long does a website take?

Landing pages take 3–5 days, business sites 1–2 weeks, Growth sites 3–4 weeks, and custom platforms 4–8 weeks or more.

Do you also host it?

We set up reliable hosting and can manage it on a care plan, or hand it over to your IT team.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We keep what works, fix speed and mobile problems, and give it a new look. Redesigns are priced like the matching package.
