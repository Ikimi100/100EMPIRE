The short answer

Photo-only wedding coverage in Abuja starts at ₦150,000 for up to 5 hours. Photo and video together start at ₦350,000, a full-day cinematic package is ₦700,000, and luxury coverage runs from ₦1,200,000 to ₦2,500,000 and above. These are 100EMPIRE’s 2026 prices.

What changes the price

  • Hours of coverage. Five hours covers a ceremony and portraits. A full day adds preparations, the reception and the send-off.
  • Photo only, or photo and video. Video needs its own operator and editing time.
  • Team size. A second photographer or videographer catches both families, guests and details at the same time.
  • Two events. A traditional and a white wedding, especially on different days, need more hours.
  • Extras. Drone, same-day edits, livestream, albums and prints are priced on top.
  • Travel. Weddings in the FCT and Niger State are covered. Elsewhere, transport and accommodation are added at cost.

How to budget for photo and video

Decide first what you want to keep: photos only, a short highlight film to share, or the full ceremony on video. Then count the hours from preparations to the last dance, and pick the package that covers them.

A 50% deposit secures your date, and the balance can be paid monthly until 2 weeks before the wedding. Our free wedding budget planner helps you see photo and video next to venue, food and everything else.

When you get your photos and films

Sneak peeks within 72 hours, the full gallery in 2–3 weeks, and the films in 3–6 weeks depending on the package.
