About 100EMPIRE

Intelligence, made visible.

100EMPIRE is a creative media and technology house founded in 2020 in Suleja, Niger State. We help businesses, ministries, couples, authors and movements look as good as the work they do.

Our story

It started with graphic design and picture framing at Hali Plaza, Kwata. Clients kept asking for more, so the studio grew to meet them.

Flyers led to logos, logos to brand identities, and brand identities to websites. Church graphics led to sermon clips, livestreams and full media departments. Wedding portraits led to films, albums and stationery. Writing sermon notes led to ghostwriting full books.

Today 100EMPIRE runs twelve divisions with one team and one standard. We serve Suleja, Abuja and Niger State in person, and clients across Nigeria and abroad online.

Founder

Ikimi Clifford Festus.

Ikimi is an ICT professional and creative director. He works across web development, graphic design, video editing, animation, photography, cinematography and digital strategy, and writes and ghostwrites books.

He leads media and communications for Unity Worship Centre Youth in Abuja, builds websites and apps for churches and organisations, and plans digital campaigns for a grassroots civic movement organised across Nigeria’s 774 local government areas.
