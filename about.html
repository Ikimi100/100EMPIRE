About

One mind.
Many crafts.

Intelligence, made visible. The person behind 100EMPIRE, how the studio started and how I work.

The person

Behind the
empire.

I’m Ikimi Clifford Festus, an ICT professional and creative director based in Suleja, Niger State.

I work across web development, graphic design, video editing, animation, photography, cinematography, audio and digital strategy, and I write and ghostwrite books. I lead media and communications for Unity Worship Centre Youth in Abuja, build websites and apps for churches and organisations, and plan digital campaigns for a grassroots civic movement organised across Nigeria’s 774 local government areas.

100EMPIRE started with graphic design and picture framing at Hali Plaza, Kwata. Flyers led to logos, logos to brand identities, and brand identities to websites. Church graphics led to sermon clips and livestreams. Wedding portraits led to films and albums. Writing sermon notes led to ghostwriting full books.

Process

From idea
to execution.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We talk through the idea, who it is for, the deadline and the budget, usually on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    Conceptualise

    I set the creative direction and the best way to execute it, then send a written quote.

  3. 03

    Create

    The approved concept becomes the design, the film, the photographs, the words or the website.

  4. 04

    Refine

    You review drafts and previews, and agreed changes are made within your revision rounds.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Final files, prints, links or launch, organised and ready to use on day one.
