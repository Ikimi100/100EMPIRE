Questions

Before you book.

How do I send large footage?

Google Drive and WeTransfer work best. If your files are very large, bring a flash drive or hard drive to the studio in Suleja.

Which sizes do you export?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, square for feeds, and 16:9 for YouTube and screens. Each package says which sizes are included.

Can you also shoot the video?

Yes. See the shooting rates on this page or the Photography & Film division for full production.

What about music copyright?

We use licensed or royalty-free music where possible and tell you if a song you request is likely to be blocked on YouTube or Facebook.

Do unused videos roll over?

No. Monthly plans are built around a weekly rhythm, so unused slots expire at the end of the month.
